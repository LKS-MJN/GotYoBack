# Portfolio design rules (standing)

These apply to every future change on this site. Do not add back what was removed without asking first.

## 1. No decorative microcopy
Never scatter small mono labels, corner texts, scroll hints ("scroll to explore"),
tech-name confetti (tickers, chips, meta lists), or any other tiny text that decorates
instead of informing. If a line of small text does not carry unique information, delete it.

## 2. Allowed small text (functional only)
- Navigation links
- Dates and periods (journey, education)
- Stat labels (they explain a number)
- Button labels
- Status words that change meaning (e.g. ONGOING)
- Footer legal line, live clock, back-to-top link
- Email address (copy target)
- Area labels inside the Toolbox lists (Programming, Design and CAD, and so on)
- No section eyebrows or kicker tags. Section headers are titles only.

## 3. No tag chips
Stack/skill chips (bordered REACT / VITE style tags) are banned site-wide.
Skills live in the Toolbox section as two plain lists, What I know and
Learning now, with no level words. Projects carry their stack in the
content data file only, never rendered as chips.

## 4. No em dashes or en dashes in visible copy
Use colons, periods, commas, or the word "to" for ranges. Pipes are allowed
in the document title only.

## 5. Real facts only
Every claim must trace to the CV or something the owner confirmed.
No invented stats, roles, or timelines. Placeholders must be marked TODO.

## 6. Bold facts, not everything
Key facts and figures in body copy are wrapped in **double asterisks** in
content.ts and rendered bold. Bold only the fact itself, not whole sentences.

## 7. Nav pill is desktop-only
The fixed bottom nav exists on md screens and up only. Do not re-add a
fixed bottom bar on mobile; the owner asked for it to be gone.

## 8. No arrow icons on buttons
Buttons and button-style links carry text labels only. The accordion
chevron stays because it shows open/closed state; mail, download, and
copy icons stay because they label an action, not decoration.
