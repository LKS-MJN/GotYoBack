# Design System Reference

## Color Palette
- cream: 50-500 (warm beige neutrals)
- red: 50-900 (primary brand accent, #c93b1d at 500)
- brand: 50-900 (secondary orange accent, #c2410c at 500)
- gold: 100-500 (warm gold for highlights)
- warm: 50-900 (text colors, #1c1917 at 900)

## Typography Classes
- `.heading-xl` - Hero titles, clamp(1.75rem, 4.5vw, 3rem)
- `.heading-lg` - Section headings, text-2xl → lg:text-[34px]
- `.heading-md` - Subsection headings
- `.heading-sm` - Small headings/titles
- `.subtitle-text` - Section descriptions, text-[15px] base, max-w-2xl

## Glass Utility Classes
- `.glass` - Transparent glass with blur
- `.glass-dark` - Dark transparent glass
- `.glass-card` - White glass with blur
- `.glass-card-solid` - Opaque white glass card

## Animation Classes
- `.animate-fade-in-up`, `.animate-fade-in-left`, `.animate-fade-in-right`
- `.animate-shimmer`, `.animate-float`, `.animate-marquee`
- `.animate-pulse-glow`, `.animate-border-glow`
- `.stagger-1` through `.stagger-4` for animation delays
