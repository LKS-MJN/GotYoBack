# Copywriting Skill

Write clear, persuasive, and SEO-friendly copy for the Mannat Overseas Consultants website.

## Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Professional, warm, trustworthy, accessible
- **Style**: Clear and direct, avoid jargon, use short paragraphs
- **Key themes**: 26+ years of experience, transparent guidance, high success rate
- **Target audience**: Indian students and families in Punjab seeking study abroad and immigration services

## SEO Guidelines

- Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, at least one H2, and conclusion
- Paragraphs of 3-5 sentences; varied sentence length
- Internal links to key service pages (study-abroad, immigration, contact)
- FAQ section for featured snippet opportunities
- Meta descriptions 150-160 characters with primary keyword and CTA

## Content Types

### Homepage Copy
- Focus on trust signals and clear value proposition
- Highlight 26+ years and 2,000+ visas approved
- CTA: Book Free Consultation

### Service Pages
- Explain process clearly step by step
- Address common concerns and questions
- Include country-specific details for study/immigration

### Landing Pages
- Lead with benefit-driven headlines
- Use bullet points for key features
- Strong, action-oriented CTA
